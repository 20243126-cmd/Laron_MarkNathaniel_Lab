Welcome to the Pokémon Gym Leaders

Kanto Region

Kanto is the first main region introduced in the Pokémon world. It is home to many famous cities, Pokémon Gyms, and Gym Leaders. Trainers travel across Kanto to earn Gym Badges and eventually challenge the Pokémon League.

Johto Region

Johto is the region introduced after Kanto. It has its own cities, Pokémon Gyms, and Gym Leaders. Johto is also known for its connection to Kanto and for introducing many new Pokémon.
